# Workflow Notes

The accountable auditor owns every workflow and final decision.
The platform records work and enforces approval gates.

## Workflow Order

1. [Engagement Setup](workflows/engagement-setup.md)
2. [Field Evidence Capture](workflows/field-evidence-capture.md)
3. [Evidence Review](workflows/evidence-review.md)
4. [Relationship Review](workflows/relationship-review.md)
5. [MATE Assessment](workflows/mate-assessment.md)
6. [Conclusion Review](workflows/conclusion-review.md)

Each workflow has a separate specification. Do not combine them into one automatic process.

## Data And Approval Controls

Use fictional, public, or AuditCo-owned test data only.
Do not store evidence files or local databases in this repository.
`REVIEWED` does not mean that a relationship or conclusion is approved.
The system records approval history. It does not replace auditor judgement.
