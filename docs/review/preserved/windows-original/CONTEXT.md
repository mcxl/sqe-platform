# ACE Auditor Workbench

ACE is the private workbench for an accountable auditor. It keeps the review scope, source evidence, relationships and professional decisions traceable.

## Review Scope

**Engagement**:
One agreed body of audit work with a defined purpose, scope and authority.
_Avoid_: Project, case, job

**Audit Question**:
A question that the engagement must answer for one control. It can address control implementation or control effectiveness.
_Avoid_: Prompt, query

## Governance Chain

**Binding Obligation**:
A requirement from an identified instrument and clause that applies to the engagement scope.
_Avoid_: General requirement, rule

**Risk**:
A defined safety risk that the engagement examines in relation to a binding obligation.
_Avoid_: Issue, concern

**Control**:
A governance measure designed to manage a defined risk.
_Avoid_: Action, evidence

**Risk-Control Relationship**:
A versioned link between one risk and one control. Risks and controls can have several links, and the auditor decides on each link separately.
_Avoid_: Fixed risk owner, automatic control match

**Accountable Role**:
The stable job role accountable for a control or another defined subject. Accountability does not move when the named role-holder changes.
_Avoid_: Owner, named person, evidence owner

**Role Assignment**:
A dated record that identifies the named person who held an accountable role during a defined period.
_Avoid_: Accountable role, permanent owner

## Evidence

**Source**:
The original document, record, photograph or other material from which an evidence item comes.
_Avoid_: Evidence link, attachment

**Evidence Item**:
A controlled record that identifies its source, origin, version or date, location and freshness.
_Avoid_: File, proof

**Evidence Provider**:
The person, role or organisation that supplied the source material.
_Avoid_: Owner

**Evidence Relationship**:
A versioned link from one evidence item to an audit question or another review record. One evidence item can have several independently reviewed relationships.
_Avoid_: Direct owner link, automatic link

**Evidence Relevance**:
The auditor's classification that an evidence item supports, weakens or contradicts one audit question. Each classification needs a rationale.
_Avoid_: Good evidence, bad evidence

**Evidence Review**:
The auditor's recorded assessment of relevance, gaps, contradictions and sufficiency for one audit question.
_Avoid_: Evidence approval

**Reviewed Evidence Item**:
An evidence item that the auditor inspected and recorded. This status does not approve a relationship or conclusion.
_Avoid_: Approved evidence

**Evidence Gap**:
Missing, unavailable, stale or inadequate evidence that can affect an audit question or conclusion. Its disposition is open, resolved or accepted limitation.
_Avoid_: Evidence item, automatic finding, failure

## Relationships And Decisions

**Proposed Relationship**:
A versioned link between two domain records with source support and a stated rationale.
_Avoid_: Approved link, graph edge

**Relationship Version**:
One immutable version of a proposed relationship. A change creates a new version and keeps all previous versions and decisions visible.
_Avoid_: Edited approval, replaced link

**Auditor Decision**:
The auditor's recorded approval, rejection or change request for one proposal version.
_Avoid_: System decision, automatic approval

**Approved Relationship**:
A proposed relationship that has an approving auditor decision for the same version.
_Avoid_: Suggested link

**MATE Assessment**:
The four-part design assessment of mandate, accountability, trigger and escalation for one control. It does not decide implementation or effectiveness.
_Avoid_: Implementation test, effectiveness test, automated rating

**CONTRA Challenge**:
A recorded challenge that tests conflicting evidence, assumptions or weaknesses before approval.
_Avoid_: Finding, automatic conflict decision

**Approved Conclusion**:
A separately recorded implementation or effectiveness conclusion accepted by the auditor after evidence, gaps, contradictions and limitations are reviewed.
_Avoid_: Generated answer, draft conclusion

**Conclusion Version**:
One immutable version of a proposed conclusion. A revision creates a new version and keeps the earlier conclusion and decision visible.
_Avoid_: Edited conclusion, replaced decision

**Substantive Conclusion**:
An implementation or effectiveness conclusion other than Not Determined. It requires sufficient evidence and no unresolved contradiction or open material gap.
_Avoid_: Assumed conclusion, unsupported conclusion

**Not Determined Conclusion**:
An auditor-approved result used when evidence is insufficient or unresolved. It must state the applicable evidence limitation.
_Avoid_: No conclusion, omitted result

**Implementation Conclusion**:
The auditor's approved judgement about whether a control exists and is implemented in practice.
_Avoid_: Effectiveness conclusion

**Effectiveness Conclusion**:
The auditor's approved judgement about whether an implemented control achieves its intended result.
_Avoid_: Implementation conclusion
